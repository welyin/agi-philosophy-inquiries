## 4. 二次Gaussian与同一H的Duhamel界

令z_c(t)是同一完成H的原均匀中心，H_2(t,z)是H在该中心的二阶Taylor式。令S解

$$

$$

\le\frac{\varepsilon^2}{24}F_3(H)F_3(a),
\tag{12}

$$
\left\|\frac{\operatorname{Op}(a)\operatorname{Op}(b)+\operatorname{Op}(b)\operatorname{Op}(a)}2
-\operatorname{Op}(ab)\right\|
\le\frac{\varepsilon^2}{8}F_2(a)F_2(b).
\tag{13}
$$

$$
