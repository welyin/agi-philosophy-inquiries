This is substantive conditional algebraic source matching.

**Assumptions:** first-order gravitational fields and second-order backreaction; a small observer near a geodesic; large baseline mass with $M\ell_{dS}\to\infty$ alongside $G_N\to0$ (§3.6, (185)); the invariant static patch uses an inextendible worldline, with lifetimes scaling inversely with $G_N$ sufficient in the perturbative limit (introduction, footnote 3). Generalized entropy formulas (149), (199) use semiclassical wavepackets and retain an additional edge contribution. The broad observer generalization is argued, not an unrestricted finite-resource existence theorem.
