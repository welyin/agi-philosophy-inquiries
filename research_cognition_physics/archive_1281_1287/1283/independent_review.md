$$
E(\theta)-E_0=A_2\theta^2+cC_4\theta^4,\qquad
C_4=\frac{45}{2}\kappa^4s^2t^2,

$$
A_2=16a_{\rm kin}\kappa^2t+3b\kappa^2st+
