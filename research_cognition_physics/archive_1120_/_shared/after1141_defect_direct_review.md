$$
2\mathsf Mg+g^2=K^2
$$

$$
\{H,C_g\}=\sum_j\{K_j,C_j\}-\{g,C_{\mathsf M}\}.

$$
C_g(u,u)=\int_0^\infty
\left(\sum_j\{K_j,C_j\}-\{g,C_o\}-\{g,C_R\}\right)(u_s,u_s)\,ds.
