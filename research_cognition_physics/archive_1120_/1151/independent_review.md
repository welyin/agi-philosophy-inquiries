$$
B^\dagger K_zB=\gamma K_z+\kappa H,\qquad \kappa=3/4.
$$

$$
B^\dagger(V^\dagger T_sVT_{-s})B

$$

$$
p_+(U)-p_+(V)>
\frac12-\varepsilon_*
=\frac{9997710001}{19999500000}
